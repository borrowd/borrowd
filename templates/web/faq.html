Groups and trust

Who can see my items?

Only people in the groups you've joined can see or request to borrow your items.

How do invite someone to my group? / How do I join a group?

If you want to invite someone to your group, go to the group details page and click the "Get invite link" button. There, you will see a QR code and URL that you can share with whoever you want to invite to your group.

To join a group, you only need to click an invitation link (or QR code). If the group requires moderator approval, your request will be 'pending' until it is approved. You cannot see items shared with that group until you have been approved.

Who owns the item I want to borrow?

Borrow'd doesn't show item owners while you're browsing. You'll only see who owns an item after they approve your request.

This keeps browsing simple, reduces social pressure, and ensures sharing is always opt-in and consent-based.

How are items shared with groups?

When you add an item to Borrow'd, you can choose whether you share it with all your groups, or just specific ones.

You can always change which groups an item is shown to by editing the item - the choice is yours!

Can I belong to more than one group?

Yes. You can join or create as many groups as you like. Each group has its own members and trust settings.

What happens if someone new joins a group?

For 'open' groups, you'll get an email letting you know someone new has joined. That way, you always know who can see and request your items.

For groups that require moderator approval to join, a new user is "pending" until the moderator accepts or rejects the request. If the request is accepted, all group members will receive a notification as per the 'open' group process.

Can I remove someone from my group?

Yes. The person who created a group can remove members from that group at any time.

Borrowing and lending

How do I find something to borrow?

Go to the item search page and use search or filters to browse what's available.

If you see something you would like to borrow, click on the item and request it.

If you can't find the item you're looking for, you can create a "Community request" to see if someone in one of your groups has the item you need.

What is a 'community request'?

If you search for an item and can't find it, you can create a 'community request'.

A community request notifies the members of the groups you are a part of that you are looking to borrow an item that doesn't exist on the platform.

If another user has the item that's being asked for, they can add the item and notify the requester it is now available if they choose to do so.

Users are limited to 3 active community requests at one time.

How do I pick up an item?

Once your request is approved, you and the lender coordinate pickup by using the in-app messaging or by email.

Coordination happens between borrower and lender on a case-by-case basis.

How do I get my item back?

When you accept a borrow request, you should clearly communicate your expectations for the timing on the return of the item you have lent.

You can also request the return of an item that is currently being lent out by clicking the "Request return" button on the item card when the item is being borrowed. This notifies the borrower that you are requesting the return of the item.

If you have requested the return of your item and it hasn't been returned after 3 days, you can raise a dispute.

What happens if something gets damaged or lost?

Borrowers and Lenders agree to the Borrow'd Code of Conduct and Borrower-Lender Agreement if they use the platform to share.

Borrow'd doesn't mediate disputes, so we encourage honest communication.

Talk with the lender, explain what happened, and work together on a fair solution. If a lender is not satisfied with the resolution, they can "raise a dispute", which is a metric shared on user profiles. In this way we try to create a transparent and accountable community.

What does raising a dispute do?

Borrowers and Lenders agree to the Borrow'd Code of Conduct and Borrower-Lender Agreement if they use the platform to share.

Users are expected to return items on time and in good condition. While Borrow'd doesn't mediate disputes, we do provide usage transparency metrics on user profile pages.

If you are unable to come to a positive outcome directly with someone who has borrowed your item, you may raise a dispute, which stays on their user profile to help other users make a decision about whether to lend to them or not.

What kind of things can I lend?

Most everyday items can be listed on the Borrow'd platform.

A small number of items are prohibited for safety or legal reasons — check our Prohibited Items List before adding something new.

Can I give an item away instead of lending it?

Yes, if you want to give an item away instead of lending it, you can choose to list it as an "item giveaway" when you create the item.

A lender can also choose to giveaway an item that is currently being borrowed by someone if they would like to do so.

When an item has been "given away", it automatically becomes an item that can be shared by the new owner.

Who can I lend my items to?

You should only lend your items to people you know and trust in real life. Borrow'd is designed for sharing within your own trusted groups.

Notifications and communication

How will I know if someone requests my item?

You'll get an email and in-app notification.

In the application, your item card will also show an active request so you can accept or reject it.

How do I message someone about an item?

Communication between borrowers and lenders happens through the application messaging function or by email.

A borrower can also send a "pre-request" message in the application, which the lender is able to reply to.

When you request or accept an item, the borrower's email address is shared with the lender so you can coordinate directly.

Is my address or contact information visible to others?

Borrow'd never shows your address.

Your email is shared only when needed:

  • As a borrower, when you request an item.
  • As a lender, when you accept a request.

Impact

How does Borrow'd reduce waste?

Borrow'd helps reduce waste by keeping useful items in circulation longer, so fewer new products need to be manufactured and shipped. But the impact doesn't stop with one borrowed drill or tent — it grows with every group that chooses to share instead of buy.

When communities begin treating access as more valuable than ownership, it creates a cultural shift. We buy less, use what we already have more fully, and rely on each other instead of the global supply chain.

One household sharing a few items makes a small difference. Hundreds of households sharing thousands of items makes a meaningful one. And as more people take part, Borrow'd becomes a model for how communities can reduce waste, lower emissions, and build a more sustainable culture together.
